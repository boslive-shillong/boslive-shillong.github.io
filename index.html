Bos Live: know exactly when your bus will come

Today, Ka Bos Jong Ngi passengers know the stops but not where the bus is, so they wait at the stop and guess. Bos Live shows each bus moving along the route, counts down the minutes until it reaches your stop, and tells you your fare.

What the transport department sees

Every passenger using the app also sends the department useful information: which buses are late, which stops need more buses, and what goes wrong on the street. This screen updates live as you use the app.

How it connects

GPS trackers already on the buses→Live location feed (API)→Arrival-time model, learned per road segment and time of day→App · SMS · WhatsApp · stop displays

The one thing needed from government to go live: access to the existing bus GPS feed and the route timetable.
